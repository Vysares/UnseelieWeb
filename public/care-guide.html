Care Guide

How to keep your pieces looking their best

Leather Care

All Unseelie Workshop pieces are crafted from full-grain and premium leather. With proper care, your piece will develop a rich patina and last for many years.

  • Condition your leather every 3–6 months using a high-quality, pH-neutral leather conditioner. Some types of conditioner will darken leather.
  • The more you handle leather, the more it will absorb your natural skin oils, and the less conditioning it needs.
  • Keep away from prolonged sunlight, which can fade and dry out the leather.
  • Allow it to dry naturally if it gets wet.

Cleaning

For everyday cleaning, a soft dry cloth is usually all you need. For more stubborn marks:

  • Use a slightly damp cloth and wipe dry.
  • Never submerge leather in water or use household cleaning products.
  • Allow to air dry. Don't apply direct heat.

Hardware

All our hardware is solid brass. Unplated brass will naturally patina over time.

  • Nickel-plated hardware needs no special care.
  • To prevent tarnishing on raw brass, wipe it dry after any moisture exposure.
  • Use a mild acid or brass cleaner to restore brass to it's original shine.

Suede Lining

Pieces with suede lining require a little extra care on the interior:

  • Use a suede brush to gently lift the nap if it becomes flattened.
  • Avoid moisture on suede where possible. If wet, allow to dry naturally and brush once dry.
  • Store pieces with the lining protected from dust.

Storage

When not in use, store your pieces flat to prevent creasing. Avoid plastic bags, which trap moisture and can cause mildew.
